Rules (`classify_score` + `score_flags` in `src/tennis/ingest/sackmann.py`):

- **Walkover** (`is_walkover`): the score contains `W/O` or `Walkover` (case-insensitive), **or** the score shows no point (null/empty, only `>`, `0-0 RET`, bare `RET`/`DEF`/`Def.`) **and** nothing else shows play (`w_svpt > 0` or `l_svpt > 0` or `minutes > 0`). A null score is no evidence that points were played, so it is walkover-like unless the stats or minutes say otherwise. Walkover rows stay in matches.parquet so they remain auditable; the engine drops them (CONTRACT section 2).
- **Retirement** (`is_retirement`): RET / RE / Ret'd, DEF / Def. / Default, and abandoned matches (ABD, 'Played and unfinished', 'Played and abandoned'). An abandoned Davis Cup rubber is an unfinished match, so it is treated like a retirement: never a label. The two flags are independent.
- Set scores with a trailing `?` and match tie-breaks `[a-b]` are normal completed results.

- Tour-event qualifying is in the qual_chall files, labelled with the **main event's level (A/G/M)** and the **main draw's tourney_id**, rounds Q1-Q3: 32,219 rows over 1,229 events. 1,228 of those tourney_ids also appear in the tour files; 1,228 of them with the same tourney_date and tourney_name.
- Exception: `2021-581` 'Doha Aus Open Qualies' (2021-01-10) has qualifying rows only: the 2021 Australian Open qualifying, played in Doha under its own tourney_id. Its rows are qualifying, so they update state but are never training rows.
- Challenger qualifying is level `C` with Q rounds under the Challenger's tourney_id.
- The tour files contain no Q-round rows.
- Qualifying rows with `is_training_level=True`: **0** (must be 0).
- Q rounds have the lowest round_rank, so within an event qualifying sorts before the main draw (same tourney_id and tourney_date).

### Match order and `order_key`

matches.parquet is stored sorted by (tourney_date, tourney_id, round_rank, match_num, match_id), and `order_key` is the 0-based int64 position in that order (CONTRACT section 1).
- Stored order equals the sort and order_key equals the position: **True**.
- Rows that tie on (tourney_date, tourney_id, round_rank, match_num), i.e. that need match_id to be ordered: **0**.
